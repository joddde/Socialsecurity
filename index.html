Social Security Break-Even Calculator

Important Note: If you don't know your estimated benefit at ages 62 and 67, go to the Social Security website at https://secure.ssa.gov/https://secure.ssa.gov/ to find out. Current Social Security projections indicate that by 2035, the trust fund will only be able to pay approximately 80% of scheduled benefits. This calculator automatically reduces benefits by 20% for calculations extending beyond January 1, 2035 to reflect this projected shortfall.
