Social Security Break-Even Calculator

Important Note: If you don't know your estimated benefit at ages 62 and 67, visit the https://secure.ssa.gov/Social Security website. Current Social Security projections indicate that by 2035, the trust fund will only be able to pay approximately 80% of scheduled benefits. This calculator automatically reduces benefits by 20% for calculations extending beyond January 1, 2035 to reflect this projected shortfall.

Tip: If the button does nothing, open your browser console (F12 → Console) to check for messages about blocked scripts (CSP).
